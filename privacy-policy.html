Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. Transparent principles regarding visitor and client information.

1. Introduction

Mona's Beauty Salon ("we", "our", or "us"), located at Ghosia Mohala, Ghosia Park, Shahkot, Punjab, Pakistan, is committed to respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our general practices regarding information sent through our static website.

2. Information We May Collect

Because this website operates as a informational static site, we do not automatically gather personal identities. When you voluntarily submit an appointment enquiry through our contact form, we collect:

  • Phone Number
  • Optional Email Address

3. Use of Information

Information provided in contact forms is utilized solely to respond to your specific appointment enquiry or telephone communication. We do not sell or lease your personal contact details to third parties.

This static website does not currently deploy invasive tracking cookies or user profiling cookies. If third-party website performance analytics tools are integrated in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Our website contains links to external mapping services (such as Google Maps). We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices of external third-party sites.

6. Data Security

We take standard precautions to safeguard any client contact details communicated directly to us. However, please note that transmission over the open internet is never completely secure.

7. Updates to This Policy

We reserve the right to amend this Privacy Policy periodically. Revisions will be reflected directly on this webpage.
